# Privacy Notice

Last updated: 1 August 2026

This covers the **Best Bingo (B)overlay** Twitch Extension and **livestreambingo.com**.

**Short version:** we store your Twitch name and ID so you get your own bingo sheet and can
show up on the leaderboard. That's it. We do not serve ads, no analytics, no tracking, nothing
sold or shared.

## If you play

Sharing your Twitch identity gives us your user ID and display name.
The ID keeps one specific sheet tied to you. The name is what appears on the
leaderboard and next to any space you suggest.

We never see your email, your chat or your Channel Points balance.

## Channel Points

Twitch handles paid abilities. We can't read or take your points. When you redeem
one, Twitch tells us it happened so we can apply what you asked for, or cancel it and give
the points straight back.

## Scribes and the streamer

Helpers who run the game sign in with Twitch, and we keep their ID and name so we know
who's allowed in. The streamer also lets us manage the Channel Points rewards on their own
channel, so we store that access token to do it.

## Cookies and browser storage

The overlay sets no cookies. The extension only remembers your light/dark mode and opacity
in your own browser. The dashboard sets two cookies for admins signing in, neither of
which tracks anything.

## How long we keep it

Sheets last one round. Leaderboard scores last until a scribe resets them, usually monthly,
and we keep a copy of the final standings so the prize draw can be checked afterwards.
Scribe and streamer records last until they're removed.

## Anything else

Twitch is the only outside service involved. You can stop sharing your identity any time in
Twitch's extension permissions.
If you'd like your name off the leaderboard, please email dailysilksongnews@gmail.com
